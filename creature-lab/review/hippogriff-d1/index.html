Runtime checks

  • The 23.1-second sequence follows the same specimen from standing through landing, folding and settling. The three cameras share each simulated pose. The grid is fixed in world space.
  • 2,179 consecutive stance samples at 60 Hz used one skinned contact vertex per foot. Maximum frame-to-frame displacement was below 0.001 mm on level ground.
  • Across 20,108 primary and secondary feather-tip samples, 0 lay more than 1 mm inside the posed torso. This test covers the tips. Overlapping vane surfaces, covert intersections and attachment seams were assessed visually at the capture resolution.
  • Maximum joint-distance deviation from bind length across the sequence was 2.1 cm. The largest deviation occurred during landing.
  • Animation updates across 676 captured poses took 0.30 ms median and 1.40 ms at the 95th percentile. Captures use LOD 2, 512 × 336 per view, and 18 fps fixed-step playback. The SwiftShader software adapter took 467 ms median to render, read back and transfer all three views per pose. Hardware-GPU playback rate remains unmeasured.
