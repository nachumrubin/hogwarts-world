Motion recordings

Each four-second recording shows both revisions at 20 fps, driven at 60 simulation steps per second. Both owls receive the same fixed-step action and target samples. The camera tracks the A.3 root. The flight, glide, bank and landing takes start from a common powered-flight warm-up.

Rendering measurements

One fully visible owl at 640 × 500. Each row samples 32 animated frames after 12 warm-up draws. Timing includes the draw and a synchronous one-pixel readback in Chromium on ANGLE SwiftShader. Hardware GPU frame times remain unmeasured.
