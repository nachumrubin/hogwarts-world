# Hippogriff visual approval

**Visually approved for world integration.** The production Creature Lab defaults to the approved E.3 finish for the hippogriff. The combined finish uses the facial and chest feather atlases, photographed coat detail, and layered tail cards. The original flight feathers, proportions, skeleton, and locomotion drive the final finish.

The final eye correction sweeps and shortens nearby feathers around both eyelids. A feather-only coverage guard follows the socket in rest space and moves with the head. The iris, pupil, eyelids, and blink animation use the approved E.3 surfaces and rig.

**Hardware performance validation is deferred.** The E.3 software-adapter studies are available in [the surface review](index.html). Asset credits and licenses are recorded in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
