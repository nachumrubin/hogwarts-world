Behavioral reference

The field sequence shows the head holding its bearing as the wings cycle, a curved wingtip late in the stroke, and spread wings held through brief glides. The clips run at source playback speed with independent wingbeat phases. The field excerpt starts at 6:02, with resizing and labels.
