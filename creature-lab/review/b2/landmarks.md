# Centaur B.2 anatomical landmarks

Dimensions are in centimetres, rounded to the precision shown. Equine cohort means are scaled to a 150 cm horse at the withers. Human population ranges are the 5th–95th percentiles of the ANSUR II male sample. The attachment is a design choice.

| Landmark | Reference framework | B.2 static model |
|---|---|---|
| Withers / croup height above ground | 150 / 148, scaled Campolina means | 148.4 / 147.8 |
| Chest depth / sternum clearance | Conformation guide calls for roughly equal depths | 74 / 74 |
| Back and hindquarter position | Short topline, rising from the saddle region to the croup. Construction target of 75–80 from withers to point of hip in side projection | 78 to point of hip. Croup crest about 97 behind withers |
| Forearm / fore cannon | 43 / 27, scaled cohort means | About 44 / 27 between surface landmarks |
| Gaskin / hind cannon | 52 / 32, scaled cohort means | About 45 / 32. The gaskin is proportionally shorter |
| Hoof ground contact | Four bearing surfaces at ground level. Design footprint 13–14 wide by 16–17 long | All four within 0.1 mm of ground. Each footprint measures 14 × 17 |
| Lower abdomen to shoulder | Human construction target of 44–47 from the lower abdominal attachment to shoulder joint centre | 46 vertically |
| Shoulder width | ANSUR II outside-deltoid breadth 46–57 | 53 across deltoids |
| Ribcage breadth / depth | ANSUR II chest breadth 26–32 and depth 21–30 | 33 / 24 at lower pectoral level |
| Skull breadth / front-to-back length | ANSUR II head breadth 14.6–16.3 and length 18.8–21.1 | About 16 / 22 at brow level, excluding ears and nose |
| Human–equine attachment | Centre the abdomen over the forelimb support area. Fan its posterior surface toward the withers | Lower abdominal ring at 147 high, centred about 6 forward of the mean fore-cannon axis |

Equine references are the [Campolina morphometry study, Fig. 1 and Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1590/S1678-3921.pab2021.v56.02148) and the [University of Georgia conformation guide](https://fieldreport.caes.uga.edu/publications/b1400/evaluating-horse-conformation/). Model limb lengths use external joint landmarks and are approximate.

Human dimensions use the [ANSUR II male measurements](https://github.com/hkair/anthropometric-stats/blob/main/data/ansur/ANSUR%20II%20MALE%20Public.csv). The upper body adapts the [MakeHuman CC0 body mesh](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj) with adult male shape targets.
