Replacing hash noise reduced median draw time by 494 ms. The constant-color control measures the remaining floor, geometry, rasterization and resolve cost. The 72- and 88-byte controls remove the detail input from the shader and vary only packed stride. The grouped pair keeps the same draw order and equations while specializing the feather branch at pipeline creation. Its difference includes compiler optimization. The single-material diagnostic also changes the shading workload. Specializing the feather branch cut median draw time from 1646 to 964 ms. Reducing pixel count by 75% cut full-shader draw time from 1542 to 995 ms, showing substantial cost persists at the smaller viewport. The narrower stride gave no measured advantage in the flat control. At 3.8 ms for the full shader, readback explains little of the historical regression. These timings overlap and cannot be added.

E.2 shares a 512 × 512 RGBA surface map across tissues and uses mipmaps for distant detail. The native studio specializes clay, feather and remaining skin draws. The data uses the E.1 vertex layout and existing mesh counts. The approved motion regression passed for planted contacts, joint reach, gallop suspension and displacement, landing order and final folding. The habitat shader was exercised at all three LODs.
