The fixed camera holds its position and orientation from idle through landing and folding. The floor grid is spaced at one metre. Gold markers are five metres apart. The four side studies track the animal, and the two wing studies show the same powered-flight cycles from above and below.

Motion checks

At 120 simulation samples per second, all four feet were airborne for 39 of 240 gallop samples. The two measured strides covered 2.52 m and 2.50 m. Both foretalons planted 0.13 s before the hind hooves. Level-ground contact and limb reach also passed the runtime regression test.

Wing checks

In D.1, neighboring vertices on a vane could cross different wrist and elbow binding thresholds. A sampled lateral edge stretched to 12.6 times its rest length at a raised pose. D.3 binds each cross-section to its shaft station. Across 676,800 powered-flight edge samples on both wings, maximum lateral stretch was under 0.3%. Two-sided shading and four-sample edge antialiasing keep the thin margins readable against the underside. The procedural feather geometry is unchanged.

Head and wing motion matrices, root movement and fold progression were compared with D.1 during idle, alert, blink, bow, bank and folding. Maximum difference for the sampled transforms was floating-point roundoff.
