## Geometry and attachment frames

* Terrain starts as a scalar density volume. WebGPU computes densities in 4 × 4 × 4 workgroups, copies them to a readback buffer, then JavaScript extracts a surface-net mesh. The terrain builder takes finite differences for normals and sorts quads into XZ cells. The stone whale fluke is a separate, stationary mesh built by smooth-unioning tapered capsules before extraction. Either approach can join a sculpted silhouette at asset-build time. Repeating the readback and meshing for every pose would add synchronization and disrupt vertex identity for surface attachments. [Density dispatch](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/main.js#L145-L168) · [Terrain](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/world.js#L55-L82) · [Fluke](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/world.js#L442-L474)
* The lathe generator stitches radial rings along a supplied profile, with noise and rib offsets. The manta specifies leading edge, trailing edge, and thickness separately across its span. These are useful precedents for authoring a mantle profile and paired fins with explicit attachment positions. Coral's random recursive branching would discard the arm count, bilateral layout, and consistent oral surface needed by a squid. [Lathe and coral](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/world.js#L406-L428) · [Manta](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/world.js#L526-L548)
* Mesh.tube computes a tangent from neighboring centerline samples, projects a carried reference axis into each successive normal plane, then stitches circular rings with a radius function. A squid needs a persistent oral-side reference at each arm root, a fallback for nearly parallel axes, spacing by arc length and cup placement in that same local frame. The reference sponge tubes also demonstrate how an inset dark throat reads through a raised opening. [Tube and packing](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/math.js#L155-L193) · [Sponge tubes](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/world.js#L428-L441)

## Rendering, instancing and motion

* The packed vertex occupies 16 bytes, with three floating-point position components and four signed normalized bytes for the normal and auxiliary value. Fish and manta each issue one indexed instanced draw. Their vertex stages combine a shared mesh with per-instance path, size, and phase. A comparable arrangement suits bristles and cups. Their transforms must follow the specific limb or skin point, and should share stable seeded variation across LODs. Static terrain's cell-sorted draw runs assume stationary bounds and would need updating for moving animals. [Layouts](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/main.js#L79-L95) · [Draw runs](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/main.js#L398-L423) · [Animal draws](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/main.js#L473-L474)
* The manta's fourth normal component stores span position. Its wing displacement grows toward the tip, lags across the span, and rotates the normal using a local slope. This is particularly apt for squid fins under a moving highlight. Fish schools and mantas follow prescribed loops. A reactive hero creature needs state, velocity, and a target, while a walking spider needs a ground-relative stance anchor and joint solve. [Animal shaders](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/shaders.js#L502-L572)
* WGSL entry points specialize shading by material and object type. The scene uses floating-point HDR color, depth, reflection-independent highlights, procedural material variation, a precomputed detail texture and a baked shadow map. The lake shafts integrate visibility and attenuation along a depth-limited ray at half resolution before a depth-aware blur. Shadows baked at startup cannot represent moving feet. The ray shader also embeds a sea level and a world-volume extent, which must become environment parameters in a streamed lake. [Bakes](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/main.js#L302-L324) · [Light shafts](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/shaders.js#L695-L739)
* Three startup quality levels adjust instance thinning, shadows, antialiasing, water resolution, and shaft samples. Runtime cell-distance tests govern spatial detail. A creature can keep a continuous animation clock through a mesh change and decimate details in nested subsets. Locomotion state and planted-foot positions must survive quality changes. [Quality presets](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/quality.js) · [Runtime culling](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden/blob/a596354eff400d458bc2aeb9bdf5e0a9734b2bcf/source/src/main.js#L398-L423)

## Reuse decision

The seeded random generator, indexed mesh builder, tube frame propagation, and per-vertex deformation weight are separable candidates for reuse. This audit recommends independent implementation behind creature-local transforms. Copying a substantial part of the upstream implementation would require retaining the MIT notice. Fixed scene extents, static lighting, and flythrough paths are specific to the source environment. ## Visual reference targets

* **Giant squid.** Compare the lateral view to the Smithsonian anatomical illustration. Look for a long mantle with terminal fins, a distinct eye-bearing head, and two feeding clubs that extend beyond eight shorter arms. The requested magical mantle is deliberately larger than the documented animal. Use the specimen photographs to judge eye diameter, surface variation, and how much the two clubs widen above their stalks. [Anatomical illustration](https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/invertebrates/giant-squid-anatomy) · [Specimens and measurements](https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/invertebrates/giant-squid)
* **Aragog.** Inspect dorsal, frontal and low three-quarter views. All four leg pairs arise from the cephalothorax, with a narrow waist leading to the abdomen. Pedipalps and fanged chelicerae need separate silhouettes. The film's practical creature spanned over 5.5 metres and provides an artistic benchmark for a heavy body under a broad leg arch.
